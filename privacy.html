Tablet Counter Privacy Policy

Updated November 5, 2026

This policy covers Tablet Counter, also referred to as AI Pill Counter in the app and on this website.

Operator: Ilios G
Contact: iliosguma@gmail.com

Counting and local records

AI Pill Counter processes live camera images on the device to estimate pill counts. When you save a count, the app stores its image, dots, count, date, selected medicine details, and any notes or corrections in local History. Your free daily counting allowance is stored separately from History; deleting saved counts does not reset that allowance.

Photo and result sharing

Photo sharing is off by default. Photos and test results are not automatically uploaded unless you choose to turn on “Share photos and test results” in Settings → Photo sharing. You can count and use the app without enabling sharing. Turning it on starts sharing eligible new captures; older automatic captures stay private unless you deliberately select them in History.

When you enable sharing or explicitly send a report, ordinary pill-count photos and associated information can be sent to our private feedback service to review counting errors and improve the app. This can include capture times, estimated and corrected counts, visual markers and model results, selected medicine NDC/name/strength, saved notes, hand counts, app/build and device/operating-system information, and counting or Save timing diagnostics. Bad-read reports and the additional automatic reporting options can also send counting examples.

These uploads use Supabase with an automatically created feedback account identified by a random installation ID; there is no separate account-signup screen. Shared records are linked to this ID. Stock-bottle records and their reference photos are excluded from these automatic sharing paths. Photos can contain information visible in the camera image, so avoid patient-identifying or other sensitive material.

Turn sharing off in Settings → Photo sharing to pause waiting automatic uploads and prevent further sharing through these enabled paths. Existing uploaded copies are not automatically erased by turning sharing off or deleting local History.

Medicine lookup

Medicine lookup sends the product’s NDC to the US National Library of Medicine’s DailyMed service to retrieve product label details. This lookup does not require sending the camera photo or your saved History. NLM handles request and technical information under its privacy policy. Product-reference information source: National Library of Medicine. NLM does not endorse this app.

Subscriptions

Apple processes subscription payments. RevenueCat helps verify purchases, restores, and subscription access. The app uses RevenueCat’s generated anonymous app-user identifier rather than requesting an email address for a login. RevenueCat processes purchase and subscription information for subscription access, fraud prevention and subscription reporting. This identifier is separate from the Supabase feedback account. The app does not receive your payment-card details.

Exports

Reports are created on the device. When you export or share a report, the destination you choose receives that file and its own privacy practices apply.

Keeping and deleting data

Local History remains until you delete it or remove the app’s data. Uploaded feedback and purchase records are separate from local History. Turning photo sharing off or deleting a local record does not erase copies already uploaded. Shared feedback has no automatic age-based expiry; it is kept for error review and app improvement until removed through the deletion process below or earlier maintenance.

To request deletion of this phone’s feedback account and shared data, open Settings → Photo sharing → Delete shared data and account. Sharing stops immediately. The server waits at least 24 hours for existing upload links to expire, then automated processing removes the feedback account and its linked uploaded photos, reports, test results, notes and corrections. Processing may take longer if a service error needs retrying. Keep the app and use “Check deletion status” on that screen to see the server’s confirmation.

A minimal deletion receipt is retained to confirm the request and prevent old requests from restoring deleted data. It contains random account/request identifiers, a hashed receipt token, dates and status, not photos or medicine details. Aggregate totals that no longer identify the feedback account can remain.

This action keeps local History and your subscription. It does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete purchase records maintained separately by Apple and RevenueCat. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Files you previously exported or shared to another destination are outside this deletion action. Contact us for help with shared-data or purchase-data privacy requests.
