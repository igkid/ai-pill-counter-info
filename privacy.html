Counting and local records

AI Pill Counter processes live camera images on the device to estimate pill counts. When you save a count, the app stores its image, dots, count, date, selected medicine details, and any notes or corrections in local History. Your free daily counting allowance is stored separately from History; deleting saved counts does not reset that allowance.

Photo and result sharing

“Share photos and test results” is on by default in the current app. When enabled, ordinary pill-count photos and associated information can be sent to our feedback service to review counting errors and evaluate the app. This can include capture times, estimated and corrected counts, model results, selected medicine NDC/name/strength, saved notes, hand counts, and app/build information. Bad-read reports and enabled automatic reporting can also send counting examples.

These uploads use Supabase with an automatically created installation identity; there is no separate account-signup screen. Stock-bottle records and their reference photos are excluded from these automatic sharing paths. Photos can contain information visible in the camera image, so avoid patient-identifying or other sensitive material.

Turn sharing off in Settings → Photo sharing to pause waiting automatic uploads and prevent further sharing through these enabled paths. Existing uploaded copies are not automatically erased by turning sharing off or deleting local History.

Medicine lookup

Medicine lookup sends the product’s NDC to the US National Library of Medicine’s DailyMed service to retrieve label details. This lookup does not require sending the camera photo or your saved History.

Subscriptions

Apple processes subscription payments. RevenueCat helps verify purchases, restores, and subscription access. The app uses RevenueCat’s generated anonymous app-user identifier rather than requesting an email address for a login. RevenueCat processes purchase and subscription information and provides subscription reporting. The app does not receive your payment-card details.

Exports

Reports are created on the device. When you export or share a report, the destination you choose receives that file and its own privacy practices apply.

Keeping and deleting data

Local History remains until you delete it or remove the app’s data. Uploaded feedback and purchase records are separate from local History. The app does not provide automatic deletion of previously uploaded feedback when you delete a local record. Contact us for questions or requests concerning shared data.

Contact
