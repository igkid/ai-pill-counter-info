Updated October 16, 2026

The app license is Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement.

AI Pill Counter includes ten minutes of active live counting each device-local day. An admitted tray may finish after the allowance is used; Save or Cancel ends that tray. This version does not offer a separate photo-counting mode.

The optional Unlimited counting subscription removes the daily time limit. The US price is $9.99 per month. The App Store shows the price before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. The subscription renews monthly unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings. There is no introductory free trial in this offering.

Saving and viewing existing History remain available. A subscription does not establish the accuracy of a count; review the count and saved evidence as usual.
